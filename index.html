// Method

One formula, every route

A promotion that gives you a phone "on us" usually takes a phone you own and asks for a plan you might not otherwise buy. So every route is priced as everything you pay over the term. A phone handed to a carrier for bill credits counts as extra out-of-pocket at what Apple would have paid for it; buying from Apple simply takes that Apple Trade In value off the price.

total = monthly plan × 36
      + phone price − promo credits        (buying from Apple: − Apple Trade In instead)
      + one-time fees                       (activation · device connection)
      + trade-in into the deal              (Apple Trade In estimate of the phone the carrier takes)
